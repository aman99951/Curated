The Problem

Finding talent shouldn't
feel like a gamble.

The current freelance landscape creates friction and uncertainty on both sides. Here's why we built something different.

The business challenge

Businesses can struggle to judge whether a specialist has the right experience for a specific project — leading to costly mismatches and wasted time.

The freelancer challenge

Freelancers spend significant time competing for projects that are not a strong fit — draining energy that should go into great work.

Our approach

We focus on trust, relevant experience, project fit, availability, and clear expectations — so both sides get real value from every introduction.

How the private pilot works

A simple, human-first process. No automated systems — just honest, careful matching.

Currently operating as a private, manually curated pilot. Every match is reviewed manually. We do not claim an automated marketplace or automated verification system is live.

We review each specialist's portfolio and work history and speak with them before introducing them.

Free for businesses and specialists during the private pilot.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Who we are

Curated Talent Marketplace is an early-stage, founder-led pilot that connects businesses with independent freelancers and boutique agencies. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can contact us about it.

Information we collect

  • •Contact details — name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number.
  • •Business information — company name, project description, timeline, and approximate budget.
  • •Specialist information — primary skill, years of experience, portfolio or LinkedIn link, and availability.

How we use your information

  • • Understand your project needs or professional profile.
  • • Manually review and identify potential matches during this pilot.
  • • Contact you regarding your enquiry or application.
  • • Introduce businesses and specialists when a potential fit is identified.

What we don't do

  • ✕ We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • ✕ We do not share your information beyond what is needed to facilitate an introduction.
  • ✕ We do not use your information for unrelated marketing without your consent.

How information may be shared

Because this is a manual, founder-led matching pilot, relevant project or professional details may be shared directly with a potential match when reasonably necessary to facilitate an introduction related to your enquiry.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as reasonably needed to consider you for relevant opportunities or to respond to your enquiry, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, or withdraw consent for future contact.

Email: curatedtalentmarketplace@gmail.com

Note: This policy is written to be clear and reasonable for a pilot phase and is not a substitute for formal legal review. It should be reviewed by a lawyer before scaling beyond validation.
